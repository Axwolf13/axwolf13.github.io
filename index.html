Real-time yoga pose estimation with a hybrid MoveNet architecture

Sādhanā · Indian Academy of Sciences, Springer · 2025 · First author

Built during a computer vision research internship at NIT Puducherry. The architecture splits deliberately: MoveNet Thunder generates high-fidelity keypoints for training, while MoveNet Lightning handles inference at 30+ FPS on consumer hardware. Accuracy where the model learns, speed where the user waits.

I owned the dataset end to end: consolidated and curated 3,435 images across 11 asanas from Kaggle and Yoga-82 sources, trained the keypoint-embedding classifier, ran the misclassification analysis and carried the paper through two rounds of peer review.

Reproduction · LLMs × Graphs

MolCA reproduction: which input does a molecule LLM believe?

MolCA (Liu et al., EMNLP 2023) reads a molecule as SMILES text and as a graph at the same time. For a graduate seminar with Prof. Dietrich Klakow I reproduced its released checkpoint on two very different machines. Several of the patches it took fix bugs nobody had reported upstream.

Then the experiment the paper never ran: give the model one molecule's text and a different molecule's graph. It describes the graph's molecule in about 90% of the cases where it clearly picks one. A wrong graph does more damage than no graph at all.

  • 62.32 / 62.77 BLEU-2 on two machines. The paper reports 62.0.
  • 14 patches to the released code
  • 2.5× the cost of breaking the graph, relative to breaking the text

NLP · Shipped web service

DODI: deception scoring for Terms-of-Service documents

DODI (Digital Ownership Deception Index) scores the gap between "Buy now" marketing and "you're only licensing this" fine print in consumer ToS documents. It's deterministic and fully reproducible, built from readability, licence-vs-ownership wording and red-flag clauses. It passes its face-validity test: GOG, the DRM-free "you own your games" store, scores lowest of all ten platforms in every year.

Then I checked my own index with three LLM judges from two vendors. They barely agree with it (ρ = 0.05 to 0.26) and the disagreement exposed a real flaw: DODI can't tell an honest subscription from a deceptive "Buy" button. Version 1.1 (September 2026) counts whole words and validates against reviewed ToS;DR grades only. Version 2 reads the archived store page first: an ownership promise only counts if the store made one.

Shipped, not just analysed: a public web service (Dockerized FastAPI, tests, CI) and an MCP server on Render that an AI agent can call as a tool, tested inside a Microsoft Copilot Studio agent.

  • 10 platforms, four Wayback snapshots each, 2015 to 2024
  • 0.45 Spearman correlation with ToS;DR human grades (n = 10, not significant)
  • 3 LLM judges from two vendors checking the index

Process mining · Purchase-to-pay

Mining a real purchase-to-pay log

A process mining pass over the public BPI Challenge 2019 log: one year of purchasing at a large coatings company, 251,734 purchase-order items and 1.6 million events. I streamed the 728 MB XES file into Parquet and measured what process teams ask first. 1.7% of items were ordered only after the invoice existed. 11,938 distinct paths, of which 45 cover 80% of items.

  • 77 days median from order item to cleared invoice
  • 22% of items need a payment block lifted
  • 96% of invoice steps still done by hand

Data analysis · Economics

Shadow Correction: piracy as a market signal

Quantitative study (team of 3) testing whether piracy behaves as a market-correction signal, structured as three testable barriers. The primary one is streaming-market fragmentation, measured with the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index on Parrot Analytics demand-share data. It tracks the rise in piracy visits, though on six years of data the trend isn't significant.

Affordability was the falsification test: piracy-intent indices built from Google Trends against income per hour worked. That correlation is statistically zero, which rules out affordability as the explanation. The third test is an event study of piracy spikes after content delistings.

  • 122 countries in the affordability test
  • −0.001 Spearman correlation of piracy intent with income (p = 0.99)
  • 3 testable barriers: fragmentation, affordability, delistings

How I work

  • I benchmark before I recommend. Models get tested on realistic inputs and failure modes go in the report next to the wins. A translation model that degrades on long legal text is a finding, not a footnote.
  • Deployment constraints count from day one. Model licences, GDPR, EU AI Act obligations and what actually fits in 8 GB of VRAM.
  • Everything survives handover. It ships version-controlled and documented.
  • Where I'm still building depth I say so. Then I pick it up fast: Docker, CI/CD and cloud deployment were on that list until I shipped DODI's live scorer as a Dockerized FastAPI service with CI on every push.
